NYLL · Lean 4

A home for Lean 4
libraries in development.

NYLL is the public development home for a growing collection of Lean 4 libraries. Work in progress is published here. As parts are completed, they are extracted and published as standalone libraries.

Published standalone libraries

These repositories publish parts extracted from NYLL. See each library for its scope, documentation, and source.

  • Pro-C Groups ↗

    Profinite and pro-C groups, free pro-C groups, and completed group algebras.

  • Class Field Theory ↗

    Local and global class field theory, reciprocity, and ramification.

  • Sawin’s Totally Real Towers ↗

    Sawin’s totally real tower theorem and related results of Martinet and Shafarevich.

  • Étale Fundamental Groups and Cohomology ↗

    Étale fundamental groups and étale cohomology.

  • Jacobian Varieties ↗

    Algebraic curves, Picard groups, and Jacobian varieties.

  • Research Papers ↗

    Formalizations of research papers.

  • Anabelian Geometry ↗

    Work toward the formalization of Grothendieck’s anabelian conjecture.
