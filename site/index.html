Published standalone libraries

Explore each library’s scope and selected results, with mathematical explanations, Lean code, and references.

  • Pro-C Groups →

    The library studies profinite groups through their finite quotients, providing ways to construct maps, determine the ranks of open subgroups, and prove that presented groups are infinite.

  • Class Field Theory →

    This library studies abelian extensions of local fields, number fields, and global function fields through reciprocity, norm subgroups, and class fields.

  • Sawin’s Totally Real Towers →

    The library constructs totally real number fields of arbitrarily large degree while keeping their root discriminants bounded and the same infinite set of primes completely split.

  • Étale Fundamental Groups and Cohomology →

    This library describes algebraic covering spaces through their geometric fibers and studies the sheaves they carry, relating fundamental groups to Galois actions and explaining selected cohomological obstructions.

  • Jacobian Varieties →

    This library constructs ordinary and generalized Jacobians. Over an arbitrary field, it proves the existence of a modulus and a unique equivariant Abel factorization for maps from an open curve to a separated commutative group scheme locally of finite type.

  • Research Papers →

    These papers study how abelian Galois data detect automorphisms and how the geometry of a boundary controls finite covers over the integers.

  • Anabelian Geometry →

    This library reconstructs genus-zero curves over number fields up to isomorphism from their arithmetic fundamental groups, and proves centralizer rigidity for geometric hyperbolic curves.
